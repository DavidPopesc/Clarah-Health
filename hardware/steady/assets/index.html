    Help button

    • Need help? Press the button once. Steady counts down 5 seconds, then alerts your emergency contact. Press again to cancel.
    • After a fall the buzzer counts down. Press the button if you're OK. If you don't, your contact is alerted.
    • During an alarm press once to quiet the buzzer, or hold 3 seconds when you're safe.
    Call 911

    Who gets alerted

    Emergency contact

    Gets a push notification if you fall and don't respond, or press for help.

    They install the free ntfy app and subscribe to this channel name. Keep it hard to guess.

    Your doctor

    Off unless you turn it on. When on, your doctor gets your score, trend and gait numbers, at most once an hour. Falls go to your emergency contact.

    Saved

      Device

      Set a new baseline after a hospital stay or a change in medication, so Steady compares you with how you walk now.

      Clarah Steady is a hackathon prototype, not a medical device. It can miss falls and can't call 911 by itself. If you are hurt, call 911.
