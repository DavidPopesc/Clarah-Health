# Eucalyptus Ledger

- A disciplined clinical-dashboard direction using a calm palette: sage and eucalyptus green, warm white and ivory, and royal blue.
- Eucalyptus (`#3c5a4e`) carries the header bands; royal blue (`#2a4fa8`, token `teal`) marks actions, links and focus; sage (`#9caf88`) and its soft tint fill quiet panels and lines; ivory (`#faf7ef`) and warm white (`#fffdf7`) are the surfaces.
- Amber (warnings), red (alerts) and plum (crisis) keep their meaning and are not part of the brand palette.
- IBM Plex Sans carries the interface; IBM Plex Mono labels dates, statuses, counts, and source metadata.
- The home screen begins with a deep eucalyptus patient-summary band and treats connected data as a compact source ledger.
- Attention items use numbered rows, strong left rules, square corners, and explicit action areas instead of floating cards.
- Navigation becomes a quiet utility rail with an underline-style active state.
- Shadows, faux depth, pointer tilt, gradients, and decorative entrance motion are removed.
- Chat reads like a secure clinical exchange: restrained message blocks, clear evidence labels, and a fixed composer.
